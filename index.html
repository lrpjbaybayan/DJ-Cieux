ABOUT

Cieux is a young Igorot-Ilocano DJ based in La Union, who started playing sets early 2025 and hasn't stopped since. Her sets are a little chaotic, a lot of fun. She mixes the heavy, bouncy energy of baile funk and New Jersey club with Tagalog pop drops that make the whole room go "Wait, I know this one."

She's still figuring things out, and honestly? That's kind of her whole thing. She shows up, she plays what she loves, and somehow everyone ends up dancing.

"I just want everyone to dance. That's it. That's the whole plan."
